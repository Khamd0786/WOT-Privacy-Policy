Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Effective: 11 November 2026

WOT: War of Territories (“WOT”, “the game”) is a location-based strategy game developed by Hammad Khan (“we”, “us”, “our”). You claim territory by walking real-world loops, build a castle on the land you hold, and raid other players.

Because the game is played by walking around, it uses your location. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version
  • We use your precise location to show you on the map and to record the loops you walk.
  • Other players never see your live position. They see the territory you have claimed and your castle — places, not where you are right now.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect:

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Password — if you sign up with email. It is sent to our server over an encrypted connection and stored only in hashed form.
  • Date of birth — used only to confirm you meet the minimum age (Section 7).
  • Sign-in tokens — stored on your device so you stay signed in.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from that provider and a sign-in token that proves who you are. We never receive your Google or Apple password. If you choose Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we receive only the relay address Apple gives us.

1.2 Location information

WOT needs precise location (GPS) to work.

  • While the app is open, we use your location to place you on the map and to load the territory around you.
  • While a run is active, we record the path you walk — a series of coordinates with timestamps, accuracy and speed readings — so it can be checked and turned into claimed territory. A run keeps recording when the screen is off or the app is in the background, so that your walk is not lost. On Android this is shown by a persistent notification for as long as recording is running.
  • When no run is active and the app is closed, we do not record your location.

The track of each run is uploaded to our server, which decides what territory you claimed. We also use location data to detect cheating, such as impossible speeds or a spoofed (“mock”) location.

1.3 Game information

  • Territory you hold, your castle and its buildings, troops, resources and upgrades.
  • Runs you have walked: distance, duration, tiles claimed.
  • Attacks, defenses, scouting and their results.
  • Account status, including any suspension and the text of any appeal you send us.

1.4 Device and technical information

  • Device model, operating system version, app version.
  • IP address and request logs from our server, kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or advertising ID.

3. What other players can see

Visible to other players:

  • Your display name.
  • The territory you hold and your castle’s location on the map.
  • Your castle layout, when another player previews it before an attack.
  • Leaderboard standings based on territory held.
  • Attack and defense results involving them.

Never visible to other players:

  • Your current or live position.
  • The paths of your runs.
  • Your email address or date of birth.

Scouting warnings tell you that someone has scouted your land — they do not say who.

Please be aware: territory and castles are tied to real places. Territory you claim around your home will show other players roughly where you walk.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who run parts of the game on our behalf:
    • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication) — sign-in. Firebase privacy
    • Sign in with Apple — if you choose it. Apple privacy
    • Mapbox — map tiles. To draw the map, your device requests map images for the area you are viewing, which tells Mapbox the approximate area and your IP address. Mapbox privacy
    • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosts our game server and database. AWS privacy
  • Law enforcement or regulators, when the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if the game is sold or merged, under this same policy.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Run tracks: 90 days after the run, then deleted. Territory you claimed with a run stays on your account.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • After you delete your account: deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something (for example, records of an abuse report).

6. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your WOT account and its data at any time:

  1. Email khamd0786@gmail.com from the email address on your WOT account (for Apple “Hide My Email” accounts, tell us your display name instead).
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my WOT account”.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete the account within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, territory, castle, runs and game progress. This cannot be undone.

7. Children

WOT is not meant for anyone under 16. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not let you create an account if you are under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Sign-in tokens on your device are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including the EU, UK, California and India), you may have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent — for example, by turning off location access.

Turning off location access stops the game from working: you cannot appear on the map or record runs. Your account and territory stay as they are.

To use any of these rights, email khamd0786@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The “Last updated” date at the top always shows the current version.

1. Play safely — read this first

WOT is played by walking around the real world. Your safety is your responsibility.

  • Stay aware of your surroundings. Do not look at your phone while crossing roads, cycling, or anywhere it is unsafe to do so.
  • Never play while driving or operating any vehicle.
  • Do not enter private property, closed areas, or anywhere you are not allowed to be. Owning a tile in the game gives you no rights to the real place.
  • Obey local laws, and take care at night, in bad weather, and in unfamiliar areas.
  • Know your physical limits. Talk to a doctor before taking up more walking than you are used to.

The game is designed so that you never need to watch the screen while walking. Use the audio and vibration feedback instead.
